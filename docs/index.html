What this proves, and what it does not.

It proves deterministic policy can allow, hold, or refuse typed synthetic actions, bind one human approval to one exact request, and leave records whose digests you can recompute.

It does not provide production authorization, tamper-proof audit logs, or machine isolation, and it cannot make bad evidence true. Everything here is synthetic and runs in a browser sandbox.
